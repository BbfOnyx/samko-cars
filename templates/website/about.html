Our Quality Assurance Process

Every vehicle undergoes our rigorous multi-point certification before being listed

Computer Diagnostic Scan

Full OBD-II and manufacturer diagnostic scans to identify and disclose any electronic faults or pending repairs.

Flood Damage Inspection

Thorough interior, undercarriage, and engine bay inspection for flood damage — a critical check for all Tokunbo vehicles.

Chassis & Frame Check

Laser frame alignment and chassis inspection to verify structural integrity after any prior collision repairs.

All foreign used vehicles have verified customs clearance documentation from Nigerian Customs Service.
